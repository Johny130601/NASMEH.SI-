# Phase 6, step 1 — Contact forms and tickets

**Date:** 2026-09-09. **Status:** step 1 complete and locally accepted. This is only step 1 of the user's six checkpoints. Stop and report after this step; steps 2–6 have not started.

## Delivered behavior

`/kontakt` now has nine topic cards with matching reason choices, optional order context, reporter details/message, an unchecked privacy acknowledgment and Turnstile. Wrong/damaged deliveries can include four photos up to 2 MB each. Cancellation/change/return requests save support tickets; they do not change order status or issue refunds. The adverse-event contact topic routes to compliance, while its dedicated structured form remains step 4.

Signed-in shoppers see only their own orders. Guest lookup verifies one email/order-number pair behind a separate challenge and returns only number/status/date. Editing proof inputs clears the selection, stale responses are ignored, and submission checks current proof again. This lookup does not grant purchase, confirmation, cart or account access.

Tickets, private attachment metadata and separate staff/customer email-delivery records are committed together before SMTP. Each request key is bound to a hash of the reporter, actual content, order proof and attachment bytes; an exact retry returns the same reference, while a changed payload cannot reuse the key. Losing concurrent submissions clean up their unused files.

Photos reuse bounded image decoding, metadata stripping and WebP encoding, with generated filenames under private `support-uploads`. `/api/support/attachments/[id]` requires the submitting account or an administrator; guest photos are staff-only. Responses are private/no-store with nosniff. Ticket photos never become public product or review assets.

Routing and contact details are validated `support.contact` settings. Staff mail contains escaped details, a validated reply-to and authenticated photo links. Acknowledgements contain only the reference and a generic receipt, avoiding disclosure of private form content to an unverified typed address. Independent delivery leases and stable Message-IDs preserve retryable failures without resending a successful leg. The daily job exposes `ticketRetries` and returns 503 for actual ticket-send failures.
