## Deviations from the step 4 design

1. **The sign-off is external and open.** The checklist is the local part; every sign-off cell is empty, the IRPS provider is not chosen, the accountant has not confirmed the invoicing rules (numbering gaps, content, retention, davčno potrjevanje, credit notes) and the responsible person has not supplied the product facts. The step stops here as the design says.
2. **The audit ran on seed and e2e data**, not on production data (none exists). It proves the shape the write paths produce; the checklist says to re-run it on staging after one manual pass through each flow (step 5) and on production after launch.
3. **Schema changes happened**, although the plan header expected none for the phase: two nullable JSON columns on `Order` and three partial expression indexes on `ConsentLog` (declared in SQL only; Prisma's schema engine ignores partial indexes, so `migrate diff` stays clean).
4. **The review-boss pass was manual** (above), not the multi-agent pass the previous steps used.
5. **Owner decisions deferred to the end of the step** are recorded, not taken: D7 (the checkout step-1 capture and its notice), GTM loading on marketing-only consent, and the smaller decisions listed in the checklist.
