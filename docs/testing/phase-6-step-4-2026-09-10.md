# Phase 6, step 4 — Returns, withdrawal, complaints and adverse events

**Date:** 2026-09-10. **Status:** step 4 complete and locally verified on the Windows workstation of the [build-and-verify run](build-verify-2026-09-09.md). Steps 5–6 have not started.

## Delivered behaviour

- **Withdrawal page** `/odstop-od-pogodbe` is now a static route (the cookie-policy precedent): the CMS legal body with its draft notice, then the **online model withdrawal form** and a link to the **downloadable PDF**. The form takes name, e-mail, address, order number, receipt date (not in the future), the goods and an optional note; the order e-mail is the proof of purchase (`resolveContactOrder`, `EMAIL_NUMBER`, or the signed-in owner). It files a `Ticket` with topic `RETURN`, the new reason `WITHDRAWAL`, the model wording as the message and `details = { kind: "withdrawal", statutoryBasis, address, receivedAt, items, note }`. Staff mail lists the structured fields; the customer receipt adds only the generic 14-day refund sentence. The order itself is never modified.
- **Model form PDF** `GET /odstop-od-pogodbe/obrazec.pdf`: CRD Annex I(B) wording in Slovenian, the seller block from the `company` Setting, the hygiene exception for opened cosmetics, same Unicode font as the invoice; served as an attachment with a one-hour public cache.
- **Guarantee page** `/garancija-vracila-denarja` (CMS, LEGAL template, `reviewed = false`): the voluntary 30-day money-back conditions. Linked from the PDP buy-box pill, the withdrawal page, the complaints CTAs and the footer legal group.
- **Complaints page** `/reklamacije` is a static route: the CMS process text plus five guided entry points (`/kontakt?tema=DAMAGED`, `/kontakt?tema=WRONG`, withdrawal, adverse event, guarantee). `/kontakt` accepts a validated `?tema=` and preselects that topic; unknown values are ignored.
- **Adverse-event report** `/prijava-nezelenega-ucinka` (indexable): reporter (name, e-mail, phone, reporter type), product (select over active and archived products), **batch number mandatory** ("Natisnjena na embalaži"), purchase place/date, optional order number with e-mail proof, reaction description, onset date, ongoing and medical-treatment yes/no with details, up to four photos through the step 1 private-photo path, a required privacy acknowledgment and an optional follow-up permission. Files an `ADVERSE` ticket with `details = { kind: "adverse", … }`; routing to the compliance mailbox is the existing step 1 rule. The customer receipt stays generic and repeats the not-an-emergency note.
- **Ticket pipeline**: `Ticket.details Json?` (migration 15); the idempotency hash covers `details`; photos are accepted for `WRONG`, `DAMAGED` and `ADVERSE`; staff mail renders details by a label map (unknown keys never rendered, booleans as Da/Ne, reporter types by label, HTML escaped); the customer receipt gains a per-kind statutory note.
- **Data migration** `20260909200000_phase6_returns`: adds the column, replaces only the unreviewed "spletni obrazec je v pripravi" draft sentence (backlog B7), inserts the guarantee page when missing, appends the adverse-event and guarantee footer links when missing. Seeds carry the same content for fresh installs.
- D4 input, not code: the IRPS provider named on the complaints page.
