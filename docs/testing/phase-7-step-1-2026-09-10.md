# Phase 7, step 1 — Admin shell, dashboard, roles, 2FA, sessions

**Date:** 2026-09-10. **Status:** step 1 complete and locally verified on the Windows workstation of the [build-and-verify run](build-verify-2026-09-09.md), on top of commit `2098bb2` (Phase 6 step 6). Step 2 is next. Every number below was produced in this run.

## Delivered behaviour

- **Roles (migration 17** `20260910120000_phase7_admin_platform`**)**: `Role` is now `CUSTOMER | OWNER | MANAGER | SUPPORT | FULFILLMENT`. The hand-written migration swaps the enum type and converts every `ADMIN` row to `OWNER` in the same transaction; on the `nasmeh` development database the one `ADMIN` user became `OWNER` and the enum lists the five values. Every former `role === "ADMIN"` check (auth config, admin layout, moderation, invoice route, private media routes, order access, seed, tests) now uses `isStaffRole` or a permission.
- **Permission matrix** `lib/admin/permissions.ts` (pure, edge-safe): fourteen permissions; OWNER holds all, MANAGER catalog/promos/content plus read-only orders and review moderation, SUPPORT orders (view, notes, refunds), customers with GDPR actions, tickets and reviews, FULFILLMENT orders with fulfilment and notes. `lib/admin/access.ts` exposes `requireStaff`, `requirePermission` (throws `forbidden` / `mfa_required` for Server Actions) and `requirePagePermission` (redirects: anonymous → `/prijava`, customer → `/racun`, unenrolled staff → `/admin/2fa`, wrong role → `/admin?dostop=zavrnjen` with a notice).
- **Mandatory TOTP** (`lib/admin/totp.ts`, RFC 6238 SHA-1, six digits, 30 s, ±1 step, replay guard through `User.totpLastStep`; no external TOTP dependency). Enrolment at `/admin/2fa` shows a QR code (`qrcode` package, SVG), the manual key and the `otpauth://` link, confirms one code and reveals eight one-time recovery codes once (stored as SHA-256 hashes). The secret is stored AES-256-GCM encrypted with a key derived from `AUTH_SECRET` (`lib/admin/secrets.ts`). Every admin URL redirects to enrolment until it is complete; every admin action refuses unenrolled staff except the enrolment completion itself.
- **Two-step staff login**: after the password and Turnstile checks, a staff account with 2FA gets no session; `authorizeCredentials` sets a five-minute signed pre-auth cookie (`lib/admin/pre-auth.ts`) and `/prijava/2fa` asks for the code or a recovery code. The second step exchanges the pre-auth token plus code through the same Credentials provider, rate-limited to five attempts per five minutes per user. Customers never see the step. `/prijava/naprej` routes staff to `/admin` and customers to `/racun` after sign-in.
- **Sessions**: staff JWTs expire 12 hours after sign-in (`STAFF_SESSION_MAX_AGE_MS` in `lib/auth-session.ts`, checked on every request); `sessionVersion` stays the revocation switch. `/admin/racun` shows the 2FA state, regenerates recovery codes against a live code and offers "Odjavi povsod". `/admin/ekipa` (OWNER) lists staff with role, 2FA state and session version; adds a member (existing account promoted, new account with a one-time temporary password, forced enrolment on first login), changes roles, revokes sessions, resets 2FA and demotes to customer; an owner cannot demote or reset themselves.
- **Shell and dashboard**: `app/admin/(shell)` holds the enrolment gate and the sidebar shell (links filtered by permission, role pill, logout); `/admin` renders KPI tiles (revenue net of refunds, paid orders, average order, items per order; sessions and conversion as "n/a until analytics" placeholders), four server-rendered SVG column charts (revenue and orders per day or week, revenue by product, orders by status) with a visually hidden data table each, and the lists: recent orders, low stock (threshold Setting `inventory.lowStockThreshold`, default 5), pending reviews, coupons expiring within 14 days; date range presets 7/30/90 days or a custom span up to 366 days. Later-step routes exist as permission-gated placeholders.
- **Middleware**: the Auth.js middleware response no longer carries `Set-Cookie` (finding F1 below).

## Findings fixed during the step

| # | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Logout race.** The first full run failed the third login of the admin spec: after clicking "Odjava" the browser landed on the homepage but still held a valid session. Auth.js's middleware re-issues the session cookie (sliding expiry) on every request it inspects; the sidebar's link prefetches, still in flight when the user logged out, answered with a fresh cookie after the sign-out had cleared it. Reproduced with cookie probes; the same race can hit a real user who logs out right after landing on a page | `middleware.ts` strips `Set-Cookie` from the Auth.js middleware response: sessions are issued at sign-in and validated per request, a JWT expires at its `maxAge` (30 days for customers, 12 hours for staff) with no sliding refresh. The spec now asserts that no session cookie survives a logout and that `/racun` no longer answers 200; passed twice in a row and in the full suite |
| F2 | Horizontal overflow at 390 px on `/admin` (list tables) and `/admin/ocene` (review-settings selects) | Dashboard tables scroll inside their own `overflow-x-auto` wrapper with `min-w-0` grid sections; the selects are capped at their container width |
| F3 | The spec matched two `role="alert"` elements on `/prijava/2fa` (Next.js's route announcer) | The error message carries `data-mfa-error`; the spec anchors on it |
| F4 | The spec read the recovery codes before they rendered | Waits for the eight entries first |

## Observations carried forward

- The storefront login form (Phase 5) submits through a client-side action wrapper and therefore needs JavaScript; the new second step is a plain server-action form and works without it. Recorded as backlog B14 for the Phase 9 progressive-enhancement pass.
- Auth.js logs every `mfa_required` first step as `[auth][error] CredentialsSignin` because the signal reuses the credentials-error channel; harmless log noise, to be quietened in step 7 if it distracts operators.
- Deviation from the design: none. The two spec items the general plan left unassigned are placed as documented in `plans/phase-7.md` (dashboard here, customers and tickets in step 2).

## Operations notes

- The seeded `admin@nasmeh.si` account is an OWNER without 2FA; its first login enrols. Any other staff member is created from `/admin/ekipa`.
- Losing an authenticator: use a recovery code, or another owner resets 2FA from the team screen. A lone owner without codes needs a database reset of the `totp*` columns.
- Staff sessions end after 12 hours; "Odjavi povsod" ends them immediately.
