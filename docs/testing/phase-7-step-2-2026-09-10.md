- **Transitions**: `markOrderProcessing` (PAID → PROCESSING) joins `markOrderShipped` / `markOrderDelivered` in `lib/orders/transitions.ts`; `cancelOrder` and `refundOrder` live in `lib/orders/refunds.ts`. A refund validates the selected line quantities against what was already refunded, optional shipping and a signed adjustment, creates a `Refund` row first, moves the money through the new `PaymentProvider.refund` (Stripe `refunds.create` with an idempotency key, PayPal capture refund with a request id, the test provider in the harness), then applies `refundedCents`, the status (`REFUNDED` or, for cancellations, `CANCELLED`), the restock through `adjustVariantStockInTx` (bundle components expanded) and the timeline. A pending refund blocks a second one; a provider failure leaves a `FAILED` row. Cancelling an unpaid order voids the Stripe intent best-effort; cancelling a paid, unshipped order is a full refund with restock. The PayPal webhook skips `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.REFUNDED` events whose refund id is already recorded, so operator refunds are never counted twice.
- **Mail**: processing, delivered, cancelled and refunded notifications (`lib/email/templates/order-status.ts`) are sent best-effort after each transition with the outcome written to the timeline (`mail_sent:*` / `mail_failed:*`); confirmation and shipped mail keep their durable queues and can be re-sent from the detail screen. Customer-visible notes render on `/racun/narocilo/[number]`.
- **Permissions**: FULFILLMENT processes, ships and delivers; SUPPORT refunds, cancels, notes and re-sends; MANAGER reads; OWNER everything (`orders:fulfil`, `orders:refund`, `orders:notes`, `orders:view`).
- **Customers** `/admin/stranke` (`customers:view`): registered accounts and guest purchasers grouped by e-mail in one list with order count, lifetime value from paid orders, marketing consent and date; filters for search, has-orders and consent. Detail (`/admin/stranke/[id]`, guests at `/admin/stranke/gost?email=`): profile, address book, orders, consent history, tickets, tags and internal notes. GDPR (`customers:gdpr`): JSON export at `/admin/stranke/[id]/izvoz.json` (credentials and TOTP material excluded) and irreversible anonymisation (`lib/admin/customers.ts`): e-mail replaced, name and password cleared, addresses, tokens and cart deleted, orders and tickets scrubbed to country only, reviews unlinked, subscriptions removed, sessions revoked; order totals, refunds and consent records stay.
- **Tickets** `/admin/podpora` (`tickets:view`): the Phase 6 tickets with status and topic filters; detail with the structured details rendered by the same label map as the staff mail, private attachments through the authorized route, delivery state of both mails, status, assignee (staff only) and an internal note.

## Deviations from the step 2 design

1. Refund lines are priced at their unit price with a signed adjustment field for the operator instead of automatic discount proration; cancellations compute the adjustment so the refund equals the remaining balance exactly.
2. Order notes carry the author's name and id on the row rather than a relation, so an anonymised or demoted author never breaks a note.
3. Transition e-mails (processing, delivered, cancelled, refunded) are best-effort with a timeline record, as designed; only confirmation and shipped mail have retry queues.

## Operations notes

- A refund that the provider rejects stays visible as a `FAILED` row; the operator can retry with a new refund.
- `refundRequired` orders (money captured, stock missing) appear in the queue banner on the order list; cancel them from the detail screen to refund and release the flag.
- Anonymisation is final and also removes the customer's ability to sign in; export first when the person requests a copy.
