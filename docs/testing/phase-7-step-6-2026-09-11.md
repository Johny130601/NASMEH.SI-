## Findings fixed during the step

| # | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The first VAT schema allowed two decimals; `lib/pricing.ts` throws `RangeError` on a fractional rate, so saving 9.5 % made the cart and checkout routes fail (seen in the first browser run) | The schema, the reader and the form accept whole percents only; the reader falls back to 22 for a fractional stored value; unit cases cover it |
| F2 | The version bump re-opens the banner for every context, including a shopper mid-checkout; the banner overlaps the checkout button | Expected behaviour (a new version must re-ask); the spec dismisses the banner. No product change |
| F3 | A timed-out browser test skips its `finally` block, so two aborted attempts of the step spec left their settings in the database and six unrelated specs failed on the next full run | The spec snapshots in `beforeEach` and restores in `afterEach` (run even after a timeout); the full run repeated on a database recreated from scratch |

## Deviations from the step 6 design

1. **Zones** are the per-method country lists the checkout already parses (`shippingMethodSchema.countries`), not a separate zones object; enabling a country means ticking it on a method. Price tiers by weight or basket value stay P2.
2. **Invoice numbering** is the order number (`NS-LLLL-NNNNN`, Phase 3); there is no separate sequence to show, so the screen states the rule.
3. **Payment providers** show environment presence only; wallet toggles are Stripe dashboard settings and Klarna stays the `STRIPE_KLARNA_ENABLED` flag (planned narrowing of §14.13 "keys", AGENTS §6).
4. **SEO defaults** carry the title template, description and index switch; the OG image stays the committed `/og-default.svg` asset until D2 delivers final imagery — a per-page image comes from product media.
5. **Store profile**: the store name and logo are brand copy, contact addresses live in `company` and `support.contact`, social links are the `footer-sledite` menu (step 5) and the EN toggle has no P1 surface, so no `store.profile` row exists; maintenance mode and the legal-link mapping are on the marketing screen instead.
6. **Legal links** feed the checkout payment step and the cookie banner; the footer keeps the `footer-pravno` menu and the legal pages keep their literal cross-links (they are the legal pages themselves).
7. **Consent categories** (necessary, analytics, marketing) stay code; the cookie table, banner copy and version are data.
8. **Maintenance password** is stored as entered (Phase 1 shape read by the middleware); hashing it is listed for the Phase 9 security pass (backlog B15).
9. **PDF text** is glyph-encoded by the embedded font, so the browser run asserts the invoice route and the unit test asserts the data builder (footer and company).

## Operations notes

- Every settings change is live on the next request; there is no publish step. Money-related values (VAT, thresholds, method prices) affect new quotes only — orders keep their snapshot.
- Turn the index switch off for staging or a soft launch and back on before submitting the sitemap; the switch also sets `noindex` on every page.
- Bump the consent version after changing the cookie table or a purpose: every visitor is asked again on the next visit.
- Provider keys never enter the database: change them in the host `.env` and restart the container; the tax screen shows what the running server sees.
