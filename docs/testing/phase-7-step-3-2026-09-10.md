# Phase 7, step 3 — Products, inventory, media, collections, bundles

**Date:** 2026-09-10. **Status:** step 3 complete and locally verified on the Windows workstation of the [build-and-verify run](build-verify-2026-09-09.md), on top of commit `a64ed8c` (Phase 7 step 2). Step 4 is next. Every number below was produced in this run.

## Delivered behaviour

- **Migration 19** `20260910160000_phase7_catalog` (generated by diffing the schema against the step 2 database, plus one data statement): `Product.soldOutBehavior` (`NOTIFY` | `HIDE`, default `NOTIFY`), `Product.klarnaEligible`, `Variant.allowBackorder` and `Variant.backorderNote`, `Collection.bannerImageMobile` and `Collection.hideBannerText`, and the `inventory.lowStockThreshold` setting (5) inserted only if missing; the seed carries the same default.
- **Products** `/admin/izdelki` (permission `catalog:manage`, held by OWNER and MANAGER): list with search over title, slug and SKU, status filter, price, summed stock in the warning colour at or below the threshold, badges, back-in-stock subscriber count; a create form (title, slug, first SKU, first price) that opens a `DRAFT` product with one zero-stock variant and its initial price row; the low-stock threshold form that feeds the dashboard's low-stock list.
- **Product editor** `/admin/izdelki/[id]`: basics (title, slug, status, HTML description, SEO title and description, catalog/search visibility, hidden-deal flag as a flag only, Klarna eligibility, sold-out behaviour), badges (up to 4 with a style), merchandising (USP chips, intro, bullets, unit price, cross-sell slugs from the active products, a free-form JSON object for extra metafields where the structured keys always win), content (four accordion bodies, FAQ up to 12, education up to 6), all validated by zod schemas in `lib/admin/catalog.ts` that mirror the storefront parsers.
- **Variants**: one form per variant plus an add form: title, SKU (normalised upper-case, unique), price and compare-at (compare-at must exceed the price), cost, barcode, weight, stock, cart cap, backorder flag and note. Prices go through `changeVariantPriceInTx` (new variants through `recordInitialPriceInTx`), stock through `setVariantStockInTx`, both in one transaction; armed restock alerts are flushed after commit and the daily job remains the retry path. A variant can be deleted only when it is not the last one and not a bundle component; order lines keep their snapshot with the link cleared, subscriptions fall back to the product.
- **Media**: up to four JPEG/PNG/WebP files per upload (≤ 4 MB each), decoded and re-encoded to WebP by sharp (`lib/admin/media.ts`), stored in the persistent `catalog-uploads/products/<productId>/` directory with random names and served by `app/uploads/[owner]/[ownerId]/[filename]/route.ts` with immutable caching; alt text, kind (gallery / card / hero), move up/down, delete (row and file). The standalone start script links the directory into the standalone tree the way it does for review and support uploads; the Dockerfile creates it and compose mounts the `catalog_uploads` volume.
- **Price history** per variant (last 8 rows) and **restock panel**: counts by status (pending, confirmed, notified, unsubscribed, ready to send) and "Pošlji obvestila zdaj", which re-arms confirmed, un-notified subscriptions for stocked variants of an active product and flushes the queue, reporting armed / sent / failed.
- **Collections** `/admin/kolekcije` and `/admin/kolekcije/[id]`: create, title, slug, SEO, noindex, hide-banner-text, desktop and mobile banners (uploaded through the same media path, previous file removed), product membership from the product editor or the collection screen, manual order with move up/down rewritten in one transaction, removal, deletion.
- **Bundles** `/admin/paketi` and `/admin/paketi/[id]`: list with bundle price, component value, savings and components; builder that turns a product into a bundle shell and then sets components (variants of other, non-bundle products with quantities 1–10), the bundle price (applied to the bundle's own variant through the price-history helper, cart cap forced to 1) and the active flag, with the "vrednost / prihranek" line computed live from the current component prices.
- **Storefront rules**: `HIDE` removes a product whose variants are all at zero stock and without backorders from `/trgovina`, search and the sitemap while its PDP still answers with `noindex`; `NOTIFY` keeps the sold-out card and capture form. A variant flagged for backorders stays purchasable at zero stock: catalog cards and the PDP show the backorder note instead of the sold-out state, the cart action accepts it, `createOrder`'s pre-check and `deductOrderInventory` let its balance go negative, everything else keeps the strict rule. The Klarna instalment line honours the product flag.

## Deviations from the step 3 design

1. Media files live in `catalog-uploads/` behind the `/uploads/[owner]/[ownerId]/[filename]` route instead of `public/uploads/` (F1); URLs keep the planned `/uploads/products/<productId>/…` shape so the storefront and sitemap need no change. Step 5's media library will be a third owner of the same route.
2. Variants can be deleted under guards (never the last one, never a bundle component); products themselves are archived, never deleted, as designed.
3. The product create form takes title, slug, first SKU and first price and opens the editor for everything else; the description stays a plain HTML textarea (no rich editor at P1).
4. The bundle "prihranek" line is computed from current component prices; the Omnibus lowest-price line itself stays a storefront computation from `PriceHistory`, so the builder shows a note instead of a second calculation.

## Operations notes

- Product and collection media are in the `catalog_uploads` volume; add it to the backup set with the other media volumes (AGENTS.md §6).
- A `HIDE` product that sells out disappears from lists and the sitemap but stays reachable by URL with `noindex`; switch to `NOTIFY` to keep collecting back-in-stock addresses on the card.
- Backorder variants can drive stock negative; the product list shows negative stock in the warning colour, and fulfilment sees the same number on the order.
- "Pošlji obvestila zdaj" needs an active product with stock and only re-arms confirmed subscriptions that were never notified; addresses that already received an alert must subscribe again on the PDP.
